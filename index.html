Recovery Roadmap

The 7-Day Digital Reset Challenge

A progressive, science-backed protocol to rewire dopamine sensitivity and build sustainable screen habits.

Day 01 Dopamine Fast

Grayscale Display Mode

Turn your phone screen to black & white. Stripping vibrant app colors immediately drops compulsive check impulses by up to 35%.

Day 02 Morning Routine

30-Minute Phone-Free Dawn

Do not touch your device for the first 30 minutes after waking. Let cortisol naturally peak before flooding your mind with feeds.

Day 03 Attention Shield

Notification Purge

Disable all badges and push alerts except direct personal phone calls. Batch check message apps 3 times daily at fixed windows.

Day 04 Sleep Hygiene

Bedroom Device Curfew

Charge your phone in the kitchen or hallway overnight. Buy an inexpensive alarm clock and read an analogue book before bed.

Day 05 Intentional Friction

Dock & Home Screen Reset

Remove all social and infinite-scroll apps from your home screen dock. Hide them inside deeply nested folders or delete native apps.

Day 06–07 Deep Restoration

Weekend Digital Sabbath

Put your phone into a drawer for a continuous 8-hour stretch on Saturday or Sunday. Reconnect with physical presence, nature, and focus.

The Science Behind Screen Signal

Understanding the neurological mechanisms that make smartphones so addicting.

Variable Reward Loops

Each unlock acts like pulling a casino slot machine lever. You never know if an email, like, or breaking news story awaits, conditioning compulsive checks.

Circadian Melatonin Block

Short-wavelength blue light directly stimulates melanopsin retinal ganglion cells, shutting down melatonin production and shifting sleep latency by 1–2 hours.

Prefrontal Cortex Fatigue

Sleep debt impairs executive inhibitory control. When tired, your brain naturally seeks rapid, low-effort dopamine hits from endless vertical feeds.
