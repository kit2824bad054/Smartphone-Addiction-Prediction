Variable Reward Schedules

Every unlock functions like a slot machine lever. You never know if an alert, like, or message awaits, creating powerful Pavlovian conditioning that triggers micro-checks.

Circadian Melatonin Blunting

Short-wavelength 450nm blue light stimulates melanopsin receptors in your retina, tricking the pineal gland into halting melatonin production and delaying deep REM sleep.

Prefrontal Executive Fatigue

Accumulated sleep debt impairs the prefrontal cortex's self-regulatory control. A tired brain naturally defaults to passive, low-effort infinite-scrolling feeds.
