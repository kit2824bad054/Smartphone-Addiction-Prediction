Engine Architecture

How Screen Signal Evaluates Dependency

A machine learning methodology built on clinical digital well-being metrics rather than arbitrary screen-time thresholds.

01

Standardized Feature Mapping

Raw behavioral variables are mapped and normalized against 3,000 empirical benchmarks using z-score standardization ($\mu, \sigma$).

02

Multinomial Softmax Inference

Linear log-odds matrices compute probabilities across Low, Moderate, and High classes, balancing precision (99%) and recall (99%).

03

Behavioral Attribution

Attribution matrices dissect which specific habit drives risk elevation versus provides protective buffering, synthesizing tailored intervention plans.

Behavioral Science

The Five Pillars of Device Dependency

Why these exact metrics predict psychological dependency and sleep disruption.

Compulsive Unlock Loops

High unlock frequencies reflect habituated micro-checking behaviors driven by intermittent dopamine release, eroding sustained concentration.

Circadian Blue Light

Bedtime screen exposure directly suppresses pineal melatonin production, delaying rapid eye movement (REM) sleep and cognitive recovery.

Sleep Deficit Vulnerability

Chronic sleep deprivation compromises prefrontal cortex executive control, weakening resistance against infinite algorithmic scrolls.
